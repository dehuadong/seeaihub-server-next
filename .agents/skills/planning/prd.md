
# Product Requirements

Clarify the product contract in its existing authoritative artifact. Create or update a separate PRD when explicitly requested or when that contract needs independent ownership. Do not require a Proposal or duplicate requirements already owned elsewhere.

Follow the project's artifact registry for current and historical owners and storage locations. Reuse project formats and relevant existing PRDs; an independent PRD does not move with a linked change record's delivery lifecycle.

The PRD defines what the system should do, not how it should be implemented.

Distinguish established requirements, proposed behavior, and unresolved decisions. Preserve the user's stated intent without turning inferred preferences into requirements. A drafted PRD does not itself establish approval or execution authorization.

Use it to clarify material:

- behavior
- scope
- non-goals
- requirements
- acceptance criteria

Keep it proportional to the work.

Do not include technical design, implementation structure, or engineering choices. Those belong in the RFC or implementation.

Material product ambiguity must be resolved before the plan can become ready for implementation.

The user is the authority for unresolved product behavior and value judgments unless they explicitly delegate that authority.
